THE THEME SPINE — THE ANSWER TO CHIP SOUP (§8)
Every card carries a four-notch spine on its left edge — one notch per theme group (THE DEED · THE PERSON · THE SPIRIT · THE AFTERMATH), lit when the story holds a theme in that group — plus its lead theme by name and a +N count. 32 categories collapse to four colors and one word at card level; the full list lives one tap away in the menu (1b) and the detail rail (1d).
